Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

N-Core Workspace Tools is an internal tool used only by members of the Infintech Designs team, with their own accounts.

What we access

With the account owner's permission, the tool reads email messages (read-only), spreadsheets and analytics data that the signed-in team member already has access to.

How we use it

The data is used only to help the team member track their own work projects. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the team member's own computer. No email content is stored on any server we operate.

Removing access

You can remove access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
